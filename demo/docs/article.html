Changing your plan or billing cycle

You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your billing settings. Changes are prorated automatically.

Upgrading

Open Settings → Billing and choose a plan. When you upgrade, you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current period, and the new rate applies going forward.

Annual saves moreSwitching to annual billing applies a discount equivalent to two months free on most plans.

Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep your current features until then. Any unused credit is applied to future invoices.

Switching billing cycle

Use the monthly/annual toggle on the plan switcher. The proration preview shows exactly what you'll be charged today before you confirm.
